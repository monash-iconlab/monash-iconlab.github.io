Yizhe Wang

Postdoctoral Research Fellow

Short Bio

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the ICON Lab, Monash University, led by Dr Yihai Fang, where I also completed my PhD in Civil Engineering. My research focuses on construction robotics, human–robot collaboration, task planning and optimization, deep reinforcement learning, and augmented reality. My work aims to improve the efficiency, safety, and practical deployment of robotic and human–robot systems in structural assembly and construction automation.

Education

  • 2021–2025 PhD in Civil Engineering, Monash University, Australia
  • 2015–2017 MPhil in Engineering, University of Cambridge, United Kingdom
  • 2011–2015 BEng in Civil Engineering, University of Liverpool, United Kingdom
