Short Bio

I'm a PhD student at the ICON Lab, Monash University, led by Dr Yihai Fang.

My research focuses on construction robotics, human–robot collaboration, path and motion planning and vision-language model.

My work aims to optimize construction safety and human–robot collaborative systems, ultimately improve worker's well-being.
