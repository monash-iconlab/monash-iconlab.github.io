Short Bio

I am currently a PhD candidate in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at Monash University. My current research focuses on LLM-driven robotic structural assembly, exploring how large language models can support robotic task planning, understanding, and execution for more intelligent and efficient structural assembly.
