The Monash-TUM collaboration wins the “Best Paper Award” at the 2024 EC3 conference

July 2024

The paper “Monitoring Concrete Pouring with Knowledge Graph-Enhanced Computer Vision: A Case Study from Munich, Germany” by Fabian Pfitzner, Songbo Hu, Alex Braun, André Borrmann, and Yihai Fang received the EC3 Best Paper Award at the 2024 EC3 Conference in Chania, Greece.
