Facilities

Monash University, Clayton campus

ICON Lab is based in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at Monash University. Our research facilities support experimentation in construction robotics, sensing, automation, and digital technologies for infrastructure delivery.

Research capabilities

  • Collaborative robotics for structural assembly and construction automation
  • Vision, sensing, and field data capture for built-environment monitoring
  • Digital twin and simulation environments for construction and infrastructure systems
  • Prototype development and validation for human–robot collaborative workflows

Location

Wellington Rd, Clayton, VIC 3800, Australia
For visits or facility enquiries, please contact eng-iconlab@monash.edu.
