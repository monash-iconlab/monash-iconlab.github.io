Join Us

PhD students, researchers, and collaborators

ICON Lab welcomes motivated students and researchers who are interested in construction robotics, artificial intelligence, automation, and digital technologies for the built environment. We offer opportunities to work on cutting-edge projects with industry and academic partners.

Research areas

  • Human–robot collaborative assembly and construction automation
  • Vision-language models and intelligent task planning for robotics
  • Smart sensing, digital twins, and data-driven infrastructure management
  • Safety, productivity, and decision support in complex construction environments

PhD opportunities

Prospective PhD candidates are encouraged to review our current projects and team, then contact us with a brief statement of research interests and relevant background. PhD applications are made through Monash University’s graduate research program.

Collaboration and visiting researchers

We are open to collaborations with universities, industry, and government partners. For joint research, visiting appointments, or student supervision enquiries, please get in touch.
