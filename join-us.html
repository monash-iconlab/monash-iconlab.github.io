Join Us

ICON Lab welcomes motivated candidates interested in pursuing PhD research in construction automation, robotics, digital twins, and safety engineering. We are looking for students with strong academic backgrounds and a passion for developing innovative technologies for the future built environment.

About Monash University

Monash University is a leading research-intensive university in Australia and a member of the prestigious Group of Eight (Go8). Located in Melbourne, consistently ranked among the world’s most liveable cities, Monash is globally recognised for excellence in education and research. In the 2027 QS World University Rankings, Monash is ranked 31st globally, with Civil Engineering ranked 39th worldwide. For more information, please visit: https://www.monash.edu/

Research Themes

  • Human-in-the-loop automation
  • Human-robot collaboration
  • Safety engineering and management
  • Digital Twin for the built environment

Eligibility Requirements

Applicants should meet the following criteria:

  • Background in Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or related disciplines
  • A Bachelor’s or Master’s degree (or expected completion) from a recognised institution, with a strong academic record (equivalent to ≥85/100)
  • Prior publications in international journals are highly desirable
  • Strong programming skills are preferred
  • Experience in one or more of the following areas is advantageous:
    • Building Information Modelling (BIM)
    • Digital twins
    • Computer vision
    • Robotics
    • Large language models (LLMs)
  • Meet Monash PhD English language requirements (e.g. IELTS overall 6.5 with no band below 6.0)

How to Apply

Interested candidates are invited to send their Curriculum Vitae (CV) and Academic transcripts to yihai.fang@monash.edu
